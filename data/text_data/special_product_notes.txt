VillageTaste Foods – Special Product Notes

VillageTaste Foods regularly introduces seasonal and limited-time food products based on customer demand, regional festivals, and ingredient availability.

Winter Special Products

During winter seasons, the company prepares:

jaggery chikki
sesame ladoo
dry fruit sweets
spicy masala snacks

These products are popular because they provide warmth and energy during colder weather.

Summer Special Products

In summer, customers usually prefer:

light snacks
less oily foods
mild spice products
lemon-based pickles

Some sweets may require refrigerated storage during extremely hot weather conditions.

Festival Special Products

During Diwali and wedding seasons, special gift packs are introduced containing:

mixed sweets
snack combinations
premium dry fruit boxes
decorative packaging

Festival products are generally available for limited durations only.

Spicy Level Information

VillageTaste Foods classifies products into:

mild spicy
medium spicy
high spicy

Customers sensitive to spicy food are recommended to select mild or medium spice products.

Children-Friendly Products

Products commonly preferred for children include:

coconut ladoo
dry fruit barfi
plain khakhra
jaggery chikki

Highly spicy pickles may not be suitable for younger children.

Health-Conscious Product Options

Some products are prepared with:

reduced oil
less sugar
high fiber ingredients
natural sweeteners

Healthy product options may vary depending on ingredient availability.

Travel-Friendly Products

The following products are considered suitable for travel:

khakhra
mathri
dry snacks
chikki

These products generally remain stable for longer durations when stored properly.

Limited Availability Products

Certain homemade products are produced only in small batches because:

preparation time is high
ingredients are seasonal
handmade production capacity is limited

Customers are advised to place orders early during festival periods.

Allergy Information

Some products may contain:

peanuts
sesame
dry fruits
dairy ingredients

Customers with food allergies should review ingredient details carefully before ordering.

Storage Recommendations

Customers should:

keep snacks in airtight containers
refrigerate sensitive sweets during summer
avoid moisture exposure for dry products

Proper storage improves shelf life and product quality.

Customer Recommendation Notes

Popular customer combinations include:

thepla with mango pickle
masala khakhra with tea
besan ladoo during festivals
chikki during winter seasons

Customer support staff may recommend products based on customer preferences and dietary needs.
