VillageTaste Foods – Daily Operations Notes

Morning kitchen preparation starts at 6:30 AM every day. Staff members first clean kitchen surfaces, organize utensils, and prepare ingredient containers before food preparation begins.

Fresh vegetables, spices, flour, and cooking oils should be checked every morning before production starts. Expired or damaged ingredients must be removed immediately from storage shelves.

Pickle preparation work is usually performed during morning hours because vegetables and fruits require proper cleaning and drying before spice mixing begins.

Khakhra and snack preparation should be completed before afternoon packaging operations start. Snack batches should cool properly before sealing to maintain crispiness.

Packaging staff members should verify:

product labels
expiry dates
sealing quality
packaging damage

Damaged packaging materials should not be used for customer orders.

Daily inventory updates must include:

remaining stock quantities
ingredient shortages
damaged products
packaging material availability

The operations manager should review inventory reports every evening.

Courier pickup usually occurs between 4:00 PM and 6:00 PM. All delivery packages should be prepared before pickup timing to avoid shipment delays.

Special attention should be given to pickle containers during transportation packaging because leakage complaints may occur if jars are not sealed properly.

During monsoon season, snacks require additional moisture protection during storage and packaging operations.

Customer support staff should regularly coordinate with kitchen and delivery teams to confirm product availability before accepting urgent customer orders.

Festival season operations may require extended working hours and temporary staff support due to increased order volumes.

Employees should maintain hygiene standards at all times by:

washing hands regularly
wearing gloves when required
cleaning workstations properly
keeping storage shelves organized

Food waste should be minimized during production. Leftover usable ingredients should be stored safely according to kitchen guidelines.

At the end of each working day:

kitchen equipment should be cleaned
gas connections should be checked
ingredient containers should be sealed
storage areas should be organized properly

Management encourages all employees to report operational problems quickly to avoid delays or product quality issues.
